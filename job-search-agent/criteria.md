# Search & Scoring Criteria

Confirmed 2026-09-30.

## Target roles

- Titles: Senior Frontend Engineer / Frontend Developer, Lead Frontend Developer, Frontend Engineer, Full-Stack Engineer (React/Next.js-heavy)
- Seniority: Mid-Senior to Lead (backed by 4+ yrs incl. current Lead Front-End role)
- Stack match priority: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Angular, PWA > nice-to-have: Node.js, Microfrontends

## Location

- Base: Johannesburg, South Africa
- Mode: Hybrid only (excluding fully remote and fully onsite unless remote is explicitly global/flexible and pays well above market)

## "Great company" filter (quality bar)

A listing scores higher when it has 2+ of:
- Recognisable/reputable brand, well-funded startup, or established tech-forward company (not a generic agency posting)
- Glassdoor/Google rating ≥ 4.0 where checkable
- Clear tech stack match (React/Next.js/Angular/TypeScript) stated in the spec
- Salary/benefits stated or signalled as competitive
- Realistic requirements (not a "10 years experience, junior pay" mismatch)

Listings failing 3+ of the following are auto-rejected:
- Vague/copy-paste job description with no real stack details
- Contract recruiter spam / no named company
- Requires relocation out of SA without sponsorship mentioned
- Junior-level pay band for senior scope, or vice versa

## Scoring (0–10, used for weekly ranking)

- Stack/skill match: 0–4
- Seniority/title fit: 0–2
- Company quality signal: 0–2
- Comp/benefits signal: 0–1
- Hybrid/location fit: 0–1

Only jobs scoring **6+** get a tailored CV + cover letter drafted. Everything scored is logged in the tracker regardless, with the score and reason, so nothing is silently dropped.

## Sources

LinkedIn Jobs and Indeed, searched daily with: `("Senior Frontend" OR "Lead Frontend" OR "Frontend Engineer") AND (React OR Next.js OR Angular)`, location = Johannesburg (hybrid).

Note: LinkedIn only searched using an already-signed-in browser session (per workflow.md hard constraints — no login automation). If the browser pane isn't signed into LinkedIn when a run starts, that run logs LinkedIn as skipped and relies on Indeed alone; sign in manually when convenient to restore full coverage.
